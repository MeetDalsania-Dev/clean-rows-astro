### **\[H2\] Which titles count in a CEO and founder list**

The person running a company might call themselves CEO, Founder, Managing Director, Owner or President. Size and sector change which title is used, so you choose the titles and we agree the title rules with you before research starts.

| Title | Who it usually is | Include when |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| CEO, Chief Executive Officer | The most senior executive running the company | Always |
| Founder, Cofounder | Started the business; may or may not still run it | You want founders still in an operating role |
| Managing Director | Runs the company in many markets; at large groups it can mean a country or division head | You want company wide MDs or country MDs as well |
| Owner, President | Common at small and family owned businesses | You sell to smaller companies |
| Former, fractional, interim, advisor | Not currently running the target company | Left out unless you ask for them |

#### **\[H3\] One leader or several per company**

Many startups have two or three cofounders. Choose whether you want only the most senior leader at each company or every founder still in the business. One leader per company keeps outreach clean; every founder gives you a second route in when the first one does not reply.

#### **\[H3\] How to get replies from CEOs**

> * Lead with an outcome they own: revenue, cost, time or risk. Skip the feature list.  
> * Keep it under 80 words with one clear question they can answer in a line.  
> * Write like a peer. No flattery and no fake familiarity.  
> * At larger companies a department head often owns the budget. Add them to the brief, for example with a [VP of Sales email list](https://www.cleanrowsdata.com/lists/vp-sales).  
> * Expect forwards. A CEO who passes your email to the right person has still helped you.

### **\[H2\] CEO email list FAQ**

#### **\[H3\] What is a CEO email list?**

A CEO email list is a file of company leaders, such as CEOs, founders and managing directors, with verified business emails, titles and company details. Sales teams and agencies use it to reach the person who runs each company they want to sell to. Clean Rows builds each list fresh to the buyer's ICP.

#### **\[H3\] How do I find a CEO's email address?**

For one company, check the website, press releases and the CEO's LinkedIn profile, then guess the address from the company's email pattern and verify it before you send. For hundreds or thousands of CEOs, a list built to your ICP saves days of research. Clean Rows delivers 10,000 verified contacts in 24 hours.

#### **\[H3\] Can I get a free list of CEO email addresses?**

Free sources such as company websites, LinkedIn and the free tiers of email finders work for a few names but take time at scale. Clean Rows offers a free sample built to your own brief within 24 hours, with no card, no call and no signup.

#### **\[H3\] How can I contact the CEO of a company?**

Email works when you have a verified business address and a short, specific reason to write. LinkedIn and the company's main phone line are second routes. If you have a customer complaint, use the company's support channels first; our lists are for B2B outreach.

#### **\[H3\] What format do CEO email addresses use?**

Most business addresses follow the company's pattern, such as first name at domain or first name dot last name at domain. Small companies often use the first name only. Always verify a guessed address before you send to protect your sender reputation.

#### **\[H3\] Is it legal to buy a CEO email list?**

Buying business contact data from public B2B sources is legal in most places; the rules cover how you email people. US rules require honest sender details and subject lines, a postal address and a working opt out. In the UK and EU, GDPR also applies and B2B outreach usually relies on legitimate interest with a clear opt out. This is general information, not legal advice.

#### **\[H3\] How much does a CEO email list cost?**

At Clean Rows, 10,000 CEO contacts cost \$199, one time, which is \$19.90 per 1,000. 50,000 contacts cost \$799 and 100,000 or more get a custom quote. There are no seats or subscriptions.

#### **\[H3\] How many CEOs will my list have?**

That depends on your ICP, so we confirm the exact count before you pay. Most companies have one CEO, so the count follows the number of matching companies plus any founders you add. If your market holds fewer leaders than your package, we tell you before payment.

#### **\[H3\] Can I get more than one leader per company?**

Yes. Choose the most senior leader only or every founder still in the business. The rule is set in your brief, not assumed.

#### **\[H3\] Can former or fractional CEOs be left out?**

Yes. Former, fractional, interim and advisory titles are left out unless you ask for them. The human review checks that the rule held before delivery.

#### **\[H3\] Can I get a CEO email list as a PDF?**

We deliver a CSV or Google Sheet, not a PDF. Both import straight into a CRM or outreach tool, which a PDF cannot do.

#### **\[H3\] How fresh is the data?**

Each list is researched for your order after you send the brief, not exported from a stored database. Every email is checked with two verification tools before the file is delivered.

#### **\[H3\] Does every contact include a mobile number?**

No. We find mobile numbers for roughly 50 to 60 percent of rows and mark clearly which rows have one. The company HQ phone field gives you a second route.

#### **\[H3\] What if the list misses my brief?**

We rebuild it free or refund you. The brief you agree before payment is the standard we check against, so titles, industries, company size and locations must match it.

#### **\[H3\] How fast will I get my list?**

10,000 contacts arrive within 24 hours and 50,000 within 48 hours. A 1,000 contact list takes about 3 hours and 100,000 or more takes 5 to 7 business days. Files come as a CSV or Google Sheet.
