### **\[H2\] Buy B2B leads FAQ**

#### **\[H3\] What is a B2B lead list?**

A B2B lead list is a file of people at companies you want to sell to, with their job titles, business emails, phone numbers and company details. Sales teams and agencies load it into a CRM or outreach tool to run cold email, calls and LinkedIn outreach. A good list matches your ideal customer profile on both the person and the company.

#### **\[H3\] What is a B2B lead?**

A B2B lead is a person at a business who could buy what you sell, usually because their role, company size and industry fit your ideal customer profile. A lead becomes qualified once they show interest, for example by replying or booking a call.

#### **\[H3\] Where can I buy B2B leads?**

You can buy B2B leads from ready made list sellers, from data platforms that sell seat or credit access or from a list building service like Clean Rows. With Clean Rows you send your ICP and we confirm the count and price before you pay. 10,000 contacts cost \$199 and arrive within 24 hours.

#### **\[H3\] Is it legal to buy B2B leads?**

Buying business contact data from public B2B sources is legal in most places; the rules cover how you contact people. US rules require honest sender details and subject lines, a postal address and a working opt out. In the UK and EU, GDPR also applies and B2B outreach usually relies on legitimate interest with a clear opt out. This is general information, not legal advice.

#### **\[H3\] How much does B2B data cost?**

It depends on the model. List sellers charge per file or record, data platforms charge per seat or by credits and list building services charge per list. At Clean Rows, 10,000 verified contacts cost \$199 one time, which is \$19.90 per 1,000.

#### **\[H3\] What is a B2B data provider?**

A B2B data provider is a company that supplies business contact and company data for sales and marketing. Some sell files, some sell access to a searchable platform and some, like Clean Rows, build a list to your brief.

#### **\[H3\] How do I build a B2B lead list myself?**

Define your ICP, list the target companies, find the right person at each one on LinkedIn or the company website, then find and verify their business email. It works for a few dozen accounts but takes days at scale. For thousands of contacts, a list built to your brief saves that time.

#### **\[H3\] Can I get B2B leads for free?**

You can collect leads free from company websites, LinkedIn and the free tiers of data tools, but you pay in research time. Clean Rows offers a free sample built to your own brief within 24 hours, so you can judge the data before you spend anything.

#### **\[H3\] How many leads should I buy?**

Divide the meetings you want by the share of contacts that become meetings. For example, 100 meetings at 1 in 100 needs about 10,000 contacts. Use your own past rates where you have them.

#### **\[H3\] How fresh is the data?**

Each list is researched for your order after you send the brief, not exported from a stored database. Every email is checked with two verification tools before delivery.

#### **\[H3\] Does every lead include a mobile number?**

No. We find mobile numbers for roughly 50 to 60 percent of rows and mark clearly which rows have one. Every row has the company HQ phone field as a second route.

#### **\[H3\] What if the list misses my brief?**

We rebuild it free or refund you. The brief you agree before payment is the standard we check the list against.

#### **\[H3\] How fast is delivery?**

10,000 contacts arrive within 24 hours and 50,000 within 48 hours. 1,000 contacts take about 3 hours and 100,000 or more take 5 to 7 business days.
