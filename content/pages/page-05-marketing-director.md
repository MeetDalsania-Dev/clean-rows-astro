### **\[H2\] Which marketing titles a Marketing Director list can cover**

Marketing leaders use more titles than most functions: CMO, VP Marketing, Head of Growth or Director of Demand Generation. Company size changes who owns the budget, so you choose the titles and we agree the title map with you before research starts.

| Title | Who it usually is | Include when |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| CMO, Chief Marketing Officer | The most senior marketing executive; owns brand, demand and the marketing budget | You sell agency retainers, brand work or anything the whole marketing team uses |
| VP of Marketing | Runs marketing across the company or a large division | Always, unless you only want a narrower slice |
| Marketing Director, Director of Marketing | Owns campaigns, channels and the marketing team | Always |
| Head of Marketing | The top marketing role at many smaller and fast growing companies | You sell to companies under a few hundred staff |
| Head of Growth, Director of Demand Generation | Owns pipeline targets and paid, email or outbound programs | You sell tools or services measured on pipeline |
| Marketing Manager | Runs channels day to day, often reports to the Marketing Director | Smaller companies or when you sell channel tools |

Tell us which rows you want. We leave out the titles you do not want, such as Marketing Coordinators, Marketing Assistants and specialists. A Head of Marketing list for startups and a CMO list for large companies can be the same order with two title rules.

#### **\[H3\] Is it legal to buy a Marketing Director email list?**

Buying business contact data from public B2B sources is legal in most places; the rules cover how you email people. US rules require honest sender details and subject lines, a postal address and a working opt out. In the UK and EU, GDPR also applies and B2B outreach usually relies on legitimate interest with a clear opt out. This is general information, not legal advice.

#### **\[H3\] How do you verify the emails?**

Every email is checked with two separate verification tools, then a person reviews the whole list before delivery. Duplicates are removed before the file reaches you.

#### **\[H3\] Does every contact include a mobile number?**

No. We find mobile numbers for roughly 50 to 60 percent of rows and mark clearly which rows have one. The company HQ phone field gives you a second route.

#### **\[H3\] What if the list misses my brief?**

#### **\[H3\] Should I contact the CMO or the Marketing Director?**

Contact the CMO when you sell something strategic, such as a brand project or an agency retainer at a larger company. Contact the Marketing Director or Head of Marketing for campaigns, channels and tools. Add Marketing Operations when you sell martech.

#### **\[H3\] Who reports to the Marketing Director?**

Usually managers for demand generation, content, brand, product marketing and marketing operations. At larger companies the Marketing Director reports to a VP of Marketing or CMO. That makes the Marketing Director the right first contact for anything that changes how campaigns run.

#### **\[H3\] How do I find a Marketing Director's email address?**

For one company, check the website, press pages and the person's LinkedIn profile, then guess the address from the company's email pattern and verify it before you send. For hundreds or thousands of marketing leaders, a list built to your ICP saves days of research. Clean Rows delivers 10,000 verified contacts in 24 hours.

#### **\[H3\] Can I include Marketing Managers?**

Yes. Add Marketing Managers to your title map and we include them alongside directors and above. You can ask for them in a separate file to run a different sequence.

#### **\[H3\] Is a CMO email list the same as a Marketing Director list?**

Not quite. A CMO list holds only chief marketing officers, who sit mostly at larger companies. A Marketing Director list can include CMOs, VPs, Directors and Heads of Marketing, which covers smaller companies too.

#### **\[H3\] How fast will I get my list?**
