### **\[H2\] Which sales leadership titles a VP of Sales list can cover**

Sales leadership titles vary more than almost any other function. A Head of Sales at a 60 person company often owns the same number as a VP of Sales at a company ten times its size. So you choose the titles and we agree the title map with you before research starts.

| Title | Who it usually is | Include when |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| VP of Sales, SVP Sales, EVP Sales | The leader who owns the sales number and the sales team | Always, unless you only want a narrower slice |
| Head of Sales | The top sales role at many smaller and fast growing companies | You sell to companies under a few hundred staff |
| Chief Revenue Officer (CRO) | Owns sales, often marketing and customer success too | You sell something that touches the whole revenue team |
| Chief Sales Officer | The top sales executive at larger companies | You want a chief sales officer email list alongside VPs |
| Sales Director | Runs a team or region, often reports to the VP | Smaller companies or when you sell to frontline sales leaders |
| VP Business Development | Owns new partnerships or new market deals | Your product helps open new accounts or partners |

Tell us which rows of this table you want and whether regional leaders count. We also leave out the titles you do not want, such as Sales Managers, Account Executives and SDRs. A head of sales email list for startups and a VP list for large companies can be the same order with two title rules.

#### **\[H3\] How to get replies from VPs of Sales**

> * Lead with their number. VPs of Sales are judged on pipeline and quota, so open with a result that moves one of those.  
> * Write to their territory. The title often shows the region or segment they run. Mention it.  
> * Keep the first email under 100 words: one problem, one proof point and one easy question.  
> * Call where a mobile is found. A short follow up call two or three days after the first email gives you a second channel.  
> * Add Sales Operations for tool purchases. VPs of Sales often hand the evaluation to Sales or Revenue Operations.

### **\[H2\] VP of Sales email list FAQ**

#### **\[H3\] What is a VP of Sales email list?**

A VP of Sales email list is a file of sales leaders with names, exact titles, verified business emails and company details. Sales teams and agencies use it for cold email, calls and LinkedIn outreach to the people who own the sales number. Clean Rows builds each list fresh to the buyer's ICP.

#### **\[H3\] Where can I buy a VP of Sales email list?**

You can buy one from list sellers, data platforms or a list building service like Clean Rows. With Clean Rows you send your ICP and we confirm the exact count and price. Then you get 10,000 contacts for \$199 within 24 hours.

#### **\[H3\] How much does a VP of Sales email list cost?**

At Clean Rows, 10,000 VP of Sales contacts cost \$199, one time, which is \$19.90 per 1,000. 50,000 contacts cost \$799 and 100,000 or more get a custom quote. There are no seats or subscriptions.

#### **\[H3\] Is it legal to buy a VP of Sales email list?**

Buying business contact data from public B2B sources is legal in most places; the rules cover how you email people. US rules require honest sender details and subject lines, a postal address and a working opt out. In the UK and EU, GDPR also applies and B2B outreach usually relies on legitimate interest with a clear opt out. This is general information, not legal advice.

#### **\[H3\] How many VPs of Sales will my list have?**

That depends on your ICP, so we confirm the exact count before you pay. We build every list fresh rather than selling one fixed file. If your market holds fewer matching leaders than your package, we tell you before payment.

#### **\[H3\] How fresh is the data?**

Each list is researched for your order after you send the brief, not exported from a stored database. Every email is checked with two verification tools before the file is delivered.

#### **\[H3\] How do you verify the emails?**

Every email is checked with two separate verification tools, then a person reviews the whole list before delivery. Duplicates are removed before the file reaches you.

#### **\[H3\] Does every contact include a mobile number?**

No. We find mobile numbers for roughly 50 to 60 percent of rows and mark clearly which rows have one. The company HQ phone field gives you a second route.

#### **\[H3\] What if the list misses my brief?**

We rebuild it free or refund you. The brief you agree before payment is the standard we check against, so titles, industries, company size and locations must match it.

#### **\[H3\] Should I contact the VP of Sales or the VP of Sales Operations?**

Contact the VP of Sales when you sell an outcome they own, such as more pipeline, better win rates or faster hiring. Contact Sales or Revenue Operations when you sell a tool they would run day to day, like a CRM add on. You can include both in one brief.

#### **\[H3\] Who reports to the VP of Sales?**

Usually regional sales directors and sales managers, with account executives and SDRs below them. In many companies Sales Operations and Sales Enablement report to the VP of Sales too. That makes the VP the right first contact for anything that changes how the whole team sells.

#### **\[H3\] How do I find a VP of Sales email address?**

For one or two people, check the company website, their LinkedIn profile and the company's email pattern, then verify the address before you send. For hundreds or thousands of sales leaders, a list built to your ICP saves days of research. Clean Rows delivers 10,000 verified contacts in 24 hours.

#### **\[H3\] How fast will I get my list?**

10,000 contacts arrive within 24 hours and 50,000 within 48 hours. A 1,000 contact list takes about 3 hours and 100,000 or more takes 5 to 7 business days. Files come as a CSV or Google Sheet.

#### **\[H3\] Can I get a free sample first?**

Yes. Send your brief and we deliver a free sample built to it within 24 hours. No card, no call and no signup.

#### **\[H3\] Do you include tech stack data?**

No. We leave out tech stack data because its accuracy is not good enough to sell. Every row has the standard contact and company fields listed above.
