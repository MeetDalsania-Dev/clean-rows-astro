## What it is

A list building service does the searching for you. A list building service takes a written brief and delivers a finished B2B lead list: the people who match it, with verified business emails, ready for your CRM or outreach tool. You do not search, filter or clean anything. You read the file.

The job, in one paragraph: Most teams that need a list have three options: search a subscription database, build it in house, or hand the work to a service. A data platform is software your team works inside every day. Building it yourself is real work, one person at a time, across LinkedIn, company websites and verification tools. A list building service is the third: the work is handed over and a file comes back.
Clean Rows researches each list when you order, to the roles, seniority, industries, company sizes, locations, named accounts and exclusions in your brief. We use public B2B sources only, check every business email with two verification tools, remove duplicates and have a person review the list against your brief. If the list misses what you asked for, we rebuild it free or refund you.

What you send: a brief (roles, seniority, industries, company sizes, locations); named accounts or exclusions where you have them; the fields and file splits you need; where the list will be used.
What you get back: a CSV or Google Sheet with 12 standard fields; business emails checked with two verification tools; duplicates removed and a human review of the whole list; a free rebuild or refund if it misses the brief.

## Vague in, vague out: writing a brief that works

A list building service is only as sharp as the brief it works from. Two briefs for the same buyer:
- Vague: "Marketing people at tech companies". No titles, so the list mixes juniors with the people who buy; no company size, so solo founders sit next to 5,000 person firms; no exclusions, so current customers and competitors end up in the file; no fields chosen, so you find out what is missing after the send.
- Working: "VP Marketing and Heads of Demand Gen at B2B software companies, 100 to 1,000 staff. Exclude agencies and our current customers. Business email and LinkedIn required." Two titles with seniority, so every row is a real buyer; a size range, so a dedicated marketing hire actually exists; exclusions named, so they are part of the acceptance standard; required fields set, so a complete row means something.
The second brief is not longer, just specific. Specificity is where a list stops being a pile of contacts and becomes a list of people who will actually reply, and it is also what the free sample checks.

## Six questions to ask a list building service (checklist)

1. When is each record researched? When you order, against your brief. Not on the provider's own refresh schedule, and not from a stored export.
2. Do I know the count before I pay? Yes, and the count that matches your ICP is confirmed before payment. A count confirmed only after you have paid means you are buying a number, not a list.
3. How are the emails checked, and how many times? Business emails checked with two independent verification tools, on every address, before the file is delivered.
4. Who reviews the finished list? A person, against the brief you agreed. Duplicates are removed and the rows are read, not just run through a filter.
5. What is the standard if it misses the brief? The brief you agreed is the acceptance standard. Rows outside it, excluded companies, duplicates or missing columns get a free rebuild or a refund.
6. Where does the data come from? Public B2B sources only. No personal emails, no consumer data, and removal requests honored across future lists.
