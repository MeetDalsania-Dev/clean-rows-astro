## Intro

The main Apollo alternatives are ZoomInfo, Lusha, UpLead, Cognism, Seamless.AI and Clean Rows. Each one answers the same job, who to talk to and how to reach them, with a different price model and a different amount of work left to your team. The fixed price case is a delivered list: 10,000 contacts for $199, one time, in 24 hours.

## Why teams look for an Apollo alternative (3 cards)

1. The per seat cost. Every new hire adds a subscription line, and credits are pooled per plan with limits that expire each billing cycle. If you need a list now and then, the seats you do not use are still paid for.
2. The research is your team's work. A database gives you a search box, not a finished list. Filtering, exporting, deduplicating and cleaning the rows is work your team takes on. Each rep who searches for themselves needs a seat and a credit allowance.
3. The job is one list, not a platform. If the work is a clean file for one campaign, a subscription is a bigger purchase than the job needs. A list built to your brief, at a one time price, is the smaller one.

## The tools, one by one (each: what it is, pricing model, published starting price with source, best fit, limits)

### Apollo: the baseline
B2B data platform: a searchable contact and company database with credits for email and phone lookups, plus outreach tools such as sequences and a dialer. Plans run from a free tier with credit limits through Basic, Professional and Custom, and credits are pooled across the team. Plan figures update on the Apollo pricing page (checked October 2026); paid tiers are pricing on request there. Fits teams that prospect every day and want the search, the sequences and the dialer in one place. The line it draws: the research is still your team's job, and credits cap how much anyone can pull each cycle.
In practice: a rep opens a contact profile, runs filters for their ideal customer, exports or reveals the fields they need, then cleans the rows before they load them into a sequence. Every rep who does that needs a seat, and every export or reveal draws on the plan's credits. The platform keeps the software; the list work stays with the team.

### ZoomInfo
Enterprise B2B data provider: a large verified database of company and contact records with engagement and account level workflows. Pricing is on request; plans are usually annual sales contracts (checked October 2026). Fits larger sales organizations that want enterprise depth and account based workflows. The line it draws: sales only pricing, annual commitments, and data served from a maintained database rather than built to your brief.
In practice, ZoomInfo sits at the other end of the market from a solo seller: it buys in through a sales conversation, renews on an annual cycle, and its strength is the depth of the enterprise records and the workflow around them. For a team whose job is one campaign list at a time, that is a bigger purchase than the job needs, and the per list cost of one clean file will usually come in under it.

### Lusha
Phone and email lookup for B2B contacts, with a browser extension and mobile app. You pay for credits: the free plan includes 40 credits a month, and paid plans are published starting at $49.90 a month for the Starter tier (400 credits; checked October 2026). Fits solo sellers who need one verified number or email at a time, from the profile in front of them. The line it draws: lookups are one contact at a time; assembling a list of thousands is your job.
Lusha is the fastest route from one profile to one number. That is exactly the job it is built for: you are already looking at a person on LinkedIn and you want their mobile without asking. It is not the tool for building a 10,000 row file, where one contact at a time adds up to a full time job of clicking.

### UpLead
B2B contact database with list building, enrichment and data refresh features. Published prices start at $74 a month for the Essentials tier on annual billing ($99 a month paid monthly); the Professional tier is pricing on request (checked October 2026). Fits teams that want to build lists themselves inside a tool and keep a subscription. The line it draws: a per seat subscription, and the lists are filtered and exported by your team.
UpLead is the middle ground: a subscription database, but aimed at people who build lists in a tool rather than search one profile at a time. It still follows the same model as the others: your team does the filtering and the cleaning, and you pay for the access every month the team keeps it.

### Cognism
B2B data platform that emphasizes data quality and compliance, with contact data and intent signals. Pricing is on request; the Standard and Pro plans are sold through their team (checked October 2026). Fits teams that want a data platform with a compliance focus and intent data on top. The line it draws: sales only, per seat, and the research still happens in the platform.
Cognism's position is data you can defend: documented sourcing, quality controls and intent signals on top of the contact records. Teams that need to answer a compliance question about where their data comes from, and that will prospect in the platform day to day, are its natural fit. It remains a platform, not a delivered list.

### Seamless.AI
B2B data and enrichment platform: contact and company records, API access and lead engagement features. Pricing is on request (checked October 2026). Fits teams that want enrichment and data access through an API as well as the interface. The line it draws: no published starting price, and the research and exports happen inside the platform.
Seamless.AI is built for teams that want data in their own systems, not just on a screen: enrichment of records you already hold, access through an API, and lead engagement features around the records. If your job is to keep a CRM or a workflow fed with fresh fields, that is the kind of tool for it. If the job is one finished campaign list, the finished file is the smaller purchase.

### Clean Rows: the list building service
A B2B list building service: we research a fresh list to your brief when you order and deliver a CSV or Google Sheet. There is no database to search and no seats to buy; you pay once per list. 10,000 contacts cost $199, delivered in 24 hours. Fits teams that need one defined list for a campaign rather than a platform, and agencies that build one list per client. The line it draws, stated plainly: no searchable database, no tech stack data and no instant export tool. Every address is checked with two verification tools, duplicates are removed and a person reviews the list; if it misses the brief you agreed, we rebuild it free or refund it.
The difference is where the work sits. On a platform, the software is the product and your team supplies the research. With a list building service, the research is the product: you send the brief, we do the sourcing, the checks and the review, and the file arrives ready to import. The platform stays available if you want it; the list is the part you no longer have to build.

## Three ways these tools charge you (3 cards)

1. Per seat, subscription. A monthly or annual price for each person who searches, with credits or export limits on top. The cost grows with the team and renews while the tool is in use. Apollo, UpLead, Cognism and Seamless.AI work this way.
2. Per credit, lookup. You buy the right to reveal one phone or email at a time. Great for a single profile in front of you; slow and fiddly for a file of thousands. Lusha is the example, with 40 free credits a month.
3. Per list, one time. A single price for a finished file. $199 gets you 10,000 contacts, done, with no seat to keep paying for afterwards. This is the model Clean Rows uses.

## Pick by how your team works, not by database size (two lists)

A platform may fit if: your reps search for leads every day; you want data, sequences and pipeline in one place; you rely on intent signals or live enrichment; many people need access to the same database.
A delivered list may fit if: you need one defined list for a campaign, not a tool; nobody has time to search, filter and clean exports; you would rather pay per list than per seat each month; you run campaigns per client, one file at a time.

## FAQ (from PAA questions)

1. Is there a free alternative to Apollo?
   A few, depending on what you are replacing. Several data platforms keep a free tier with a small credit allowance, so you can search and export a handful of contacts a month. Clean Rows offers something different: a free sample of 100 leads built to your own ICP, delivered within 24 hours, no card and no call. The sample is real rows from a list we would deliver, so you can judge the fit before anything is paid.
2. What is the cheapest Apollo alternative?
   It depends on how many contacts you need and how often. A platform with a free tier costs nothing until you upgrade, but the credits rarely cover a full campaign. If you need one clean list per campaign, a per list price is usually cheaper than a per seat subscription: Clean Rows delivers 10,000 contacts for $199, one time. Compare one month of seats against one list before you decide.
3. How do I switch from Apollo to a list building service?
   You do not replace the tool, you replace the data work. Keep the platform for outreach if you want to, and buy the finished list it would otherwise be searching for. Clean Rows delivers a standard CSV or Google Sheet that imports into the CRM or sequencing tool you already use, with column names agreed in advance.
4. Which Apollo alternative supports WhatsApp outreach?
   The channel is a feature of the outreach software, not of the data. Apollo and several other platforms include messaging channels inside their own tools. A delivered list does not care which channel you pick: export the CSV and import it into whatever tool sends your WhatsApp messages, then keep the number you need in the file.
5. Why do people look for an Apollo alternative?
   Mostly the cost model and the time. A per seat subscription adds a new line for every hire, and credits cap how much each rep can pull each cycle. Filtering, deduplicating and cleaning a database export is work someone has to do. A list built to your brief hands you the finished file, so the team spends its hours on outreach instead of research.
6. Does Clean Rows include a CRM, sequences or a dialer?
   No. Clean Rows delivers the list only, as a CSV or Google Sheet. You run outreach in the email, calling or CRM tools you already use. If you need sequences, a dialer or intent data, that stays with the platform you already run; the list is the part that is done for you.
