### **\[H2\] Which finance leadership titles a CFO list can cover**

The top finance job changes title with company size: CFO at larger companies, Finance Director or Head of Finance at smaller ones. So you choose the titles and we agree the title map with you before research starts.

| Title | Who it usually is | Include when |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| CFO, Chief Financial Officer | The most senior finance executive; owns budgets, reporting and funding | Always |
| VP of Finance | Runs finance across the company or a large division, often reports to the CFO | You sell to larger companies where the VP runs the evaluation |
| Finance Director, Director of Finance | The top finance role at many mid sized companies | Always, unless you only want chief officers |
| Head of Finance | The top finance role at many smaller and fast growing companies | You sell to companies under a few hundred staff |
| Financial Controller, Controller | Owns accounting, the monthly close and controls | You sell accounting, close or audit tools |
| Head of FP\&A, Treasurer | Own planning and cash | You sell planning, forecasting or treasury tools |

Tell us which rows you want. We leave out the titles you do not want, such as accountants, analysts, bookkeepers and payables clerks. A Head of Finance list for startups and a CFO list for large companies can be the same order with two title rules.

#### **\[H3\] Is it legal to buy a CFO email list?**

Buying business contact data from public B2B sources is legal in most places; the rules cover how you email people. US rules require honest sender details and subject lines, a postal address and a working opt out. In the UK and EU, GDPR also applies and B2B outreach usually relies on legitimate interest with a clear opt out. This is general information, not legal advice.

#### **\[H3\] How many CFOs will my list have?**

That depends on your ICP, so we confirm the exact count before you pay. We build every list fresh rather than selling one fixed file. If your market holds fewer matching leaders than your package, we tell you before payment.

#### **\[H3\] How fresh is the data?**

Each list is researched for your order after you send the brief, not exported from a stored database. Every email is checked with two verification tools before the file is delivered.

#### **\[H3\] How do you verify the emails?**

Every email is checked with two separate verification tools, then a person reviews the whole list before delivery. Duplicates are removed before the file reaches you.

#### **\[H3\] Does every contact include a mobile number?**

No. We find mobile numbers for roughly 50 to 60 percent of rows and mark clearly which rows have one. The company HQ phone field gives you a second route.

#### **\[H3\] What if the list misses my brief?**

We rebuild it free or refund you. The brief you agree before payment is the standard we check against, so titles, industries, company size and locations must match it.

#### **\[H3\] Should I contact the CFO or the Controller?**

Contact the CFO when you sell something that changes cost, cash or risk across the company. Contact the Controller for accounting, close and audit tools, since they run that work day to day. You can include both in one brief.

#### **\[H3\] Who reports to the CFO?**

Usually the Controller, the head of FP\&A, the Treasurer and the heads of tax and accounting. At smaller companies a Finance Director or Head of Finance may cover several of these jobs. The CFO reports to the CEO and often works closely with the board.

#### **\[H3\] How do I find a CFO's email address?**

For one company, check the website, investor or press pages and the CFO's LinkedIn profile, then guess the address from the company's email pattern and verify it before you send. For hundreds or thousands of finance leaders, a list built to your ICP saves days of research. Clean Rows delivers 10,000 verified contacts in 24 hours.

#### **\[H3\] What is the difference between a CFO and a Finance Director?**

A CFO is the chief finance executive and usually sits on the leadership team of a larger company. A Finance Director often runs finance at a mid sized company or reports to the CFO at a larger one. Include both titles to cover every company size.

#### **\[H3\] How fast will I get my list?**

10,000 contacts arrive within 24 hours and 50,000 within 48 hours. A 1,000 contact list takes about 3 hours and 100,000 or more takes 5 to 7 business days. Files come as a CSV or Google Sheet.

No. We leave out tech stack data because its accuracy is not good enough to sell. Every row has the standard contact and company fields listed above.
