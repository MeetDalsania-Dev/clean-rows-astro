# Clean Rows SEO, AEO and GEO plan (October 2026 – January 2027)

Owner: Meet Dalsania · Site: https://www.cleanrowsdata.com · Written 1 October 2026

## 1. The goal, and what is realistic

**Goal:** first-page rankings for our 11 target keywords within 4 months, and Clean Rows cited in AI answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews).

**Honest expectation.** cleanrowsdata.com is a new domain with almost no backlinks. The broad, high-intent terms ("buy B2B email list", "B2B data provider", "B2B contact database") are held by large data platforms, review sites and "best providers" listicles with years of links. Nobody can promise #1 for those in 4 months. What is achievable:

| By the end of month 4 | Target |
|---|---|
| Long-tail and mid-tail keywords (e.g. "CEO email list", "outsource lead list building", "B2B data decay rate") | Page 1 for 15–25 of them, several in the top 3 |
| Mid-tail money keywords ("custom B2B lead list", "buy verified email list") | Page 1–2 |
| Head terms ("buy B2B email list", "B2B data provider") | Top 30, moving up |
| AI answers | Cited for at least 5 of our 20 tracking prompts |
| Leads | Organic free-sample requests every week |

Rankings follow three things, in this order of weight for a new site: **authority (links and brand mentions)**, **content that answers the search better than what ranks now**, and **technical health** (already done). This plan spends most of its effort on the first two.

## 4. Authority: links and mentions (the biggest lever)

Aim for 30–50 quality referring domains in 4 months.

- **Month 1, profiles (fast, easy):** LinkedIn company page, Crunchbase, Clutch, GoodFirms, G2 and similar listings in "lead generation services", Trustpilot, Product Hunt maker profile, industry directories. Use the same name, description and URL everywhere.
- **Linkable assets:** the ICP brief template (downloadable), a "B2B data decay calculator", and in month 3 an original study built from our own real, anonymised order data (for example, mobile coverage by job title). Original data gets both links and AI citations.
- **Listicle outreach:** AI engines quote "best B2B data providers" articles. Find the 30 that rank and pitch to be included, with a free sample for the author.
- **Guest posts and podcasts:** 2 pitches a week to outbound, sales and agency blogs and podcasts. Meet as the expert voice.
- **Expert quotes:** answer journalist requests (Qwoted, Featured) on sales, outbound and data topics.
- **Partners:** outreach tools and agencies we work with. Partner pages and integration directories.
- **Community:** genuine, helpful answers in r/sales, r/coldemail, LinkedIn groups. No spam links.
- **LinkedIn:** Meet posts 3 times a week (short lessons from real list building). Builds the author entity Google and AI engines connect to our content.

## 5. AEO and GEO: getting cited by AI answers

Already done on the site: definitive answers, FAQ schema, comparison tables, sourced stats, llms.txt, clean HTML, fast pages.

Still to do:
- **Bing Webmaster Tools**: ChatGPT search and Copilot use Bing's index. Verify the site and submit the sitemap.
- **Entity consistency**: once the profiles exist, add them to the Organization schema (sameAs) and fill the founder and author details (photo, LinkedIn, bio).
- **Be quoted elsewhere**: AI engines trust third-party mentions more than our own pages. Listicles, directories and guest posts matter as much for GEO as for SEO.
- **Answer-first writing** in every new post: the question as the heading, the answer in the first sentence.
- **Track 20 prompts monthly**, e.g. "best custom B2B lead list service", "where to buy a verified B2B email list", "how fast does B2B data decay". Note whether Clean Rows is cited and which sources are.
